# Navy and Warm-Grey Colour Refresh

## Goal
Update the existing portfolio from its cool-blue frosted palette to a calm, professional navy and warm-grey colour scheme without changing its content, layout, typography, or interactions.

## Changes
- Replace the current cool-white page background with layered warm-grey neutrals.
- Shift headings, body text, navigation, and primary actions to deep navy tones with accessible contrast.
- Rebalance cards, glass surfaces, borders, pills, timeline details, and the contact area around warm-grey surfaces and restrained navy accents.
- Update ambient background effects, portrait glow, shadows, focus rings, and hover states so they feel cohesive with the new palette.
- Keep the existing project images, portrait, Space Grotesk/Inter typography, section structure, spacing, and animations unchanged.

## Validation
- Check the full page on desktop and mobile for consistent colours, legible text, and visible interaction states.
- Confirm navigation, section scrolling, mobile menu, and links still behave correctly.
