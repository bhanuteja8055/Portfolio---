# Contact Form and Résumé Download

## Email prerequisite
- Configure and verify a sender domain owned by you through the email setup flow.
- The form can be built immediately, but messages will begin sending only after domain verification completes.

## Contact form
- Replace the simple contact panel with a responsive two-column layout: contact details and social links on one side, a focused form on the other.
- Include name, email, subject, and message fields with clear labels, sensible length limits, and accessible error messages.
- Add a hidden anti-spam field and server-side validation before accepting submissions.
- Send each valid message to the portfolio email already shown on the site: `bhanuteja725@gmail.com`.
- Use a branded email template containing the sender’s name, reply email, subject, message, and submission time.
- Show clear sending, success, rate-limit, and failure states without navigating away from the portfolio.
- Keep the existing direct email, LinkedIn, and GitHub links available.

## Résumé download
- Add the supplied résumé PDF to the site as a direct download with a clear filename.
- Add a “Download résumé” action near the main introduction and repeat it in the contact area for easy access.
- Use a download icon, accessible labeling, and browser-native PDF download behaviour.

## Design and verification
- Preserve the navy and warm-grey palette, typography, full-screen opening, sticky navigation, animations, and current content.
- Match all new fields and buttons to the existing visual system and ensure the form stacks cleanly on mobile.
- Verify field validation, anti-spam handling, submission feedback, email template rendering, résumé download, keyboard use, and desktop/mobile layouts.
